What's inside

One system, three points of view

Agents track their own numbers, leaders act on today's numbers, and management reviews the whole operation by month.

Daily, weekly & monthly dashboards

Agents see their own performance across all three cadences, so today's pace and the month's trend are never a guessing game.

Live daily production tracker

Leaders see who's behind target as the day happens, so they can step in and fix it — not find out tomorrow.

Management overview by month

A dynamic dashboard, so leadership can review agent and campaigns performance for any period.

HR, response & payout sheets

Built-in HR records, a response log, and penalties & bonuses tracking — each with dynamic forms instead of manual spreadsheets.

Advanced

Campaign cycles & teams

Track each campaign through its full cycle, and split agents into two or more teams so operations can run each independently.

Advanced

Peak-hour performance tracker

See which hours actually produce for each campaign and each agent, so operations can assign shifts around when performance is highest — and catch when someone's output drops later in the day.
